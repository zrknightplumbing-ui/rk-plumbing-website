About Robert Knight

Plumbing you can count on.

Licensed Master PlumberFrom small leaks to big repairs, installs and remodels — if you need reliable plumbing work you can trust, we’re here to help.

Robert Knight was born and raised in Sullivan County, NH. After graduating from Stevens High School in 2015, Robert began his career in the plumbing trade, building his experience through years of hands-on work and dedication to the craft.

Today, as a Licensed Master Plumber with 10 years of experience, Robert takes pride in providing dependable, high-quality plumbing services to his community. From small repairs to larger projects, his goal is simple: to deliver quality workmanship and reliable service that customers can count on, day in and day out.
